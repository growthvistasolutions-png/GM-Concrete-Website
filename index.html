Our Story

More Than Concrete —
It's Personal.

MG Concrete LLC was built right here in Kentucky — founded by a family with deep roots in this state and an unshakable belief that every job deserves honest work and real craftsmanship. We started with one truck, two hands, and a commitment to our neighbors that we haven't broken since.

The "MG" in our name is not just a brand — it stands for the initials of our daughter, who was born with Down syndrome. She is our greatest inspiration every single day. Her joy, her resilience, and her spirit remind us to show up fully, work honestly, and never cut corners — because everything we build carries her name.

We proudly wear blue and yellow. These are the official colors of Down Syndrome awareness. We carry them in our logo, our brand, and our trucks as a daily reminder of who inspires us — and why we do this work the way we do it.

When you hire MG, you're supporting a family business with a purpose — one that gives back, shows up, and brings that same heart to every single pour.
